## Project Summary

PackPair is an AI-driven team-formation tool for UW class projects. Students fill in a profile (skills, schedule, communication style, topic interests) and the system forms balanced teams using three complementary techniques: (1) a Google OR-Tools **CP-SAT integer program** over a weighted objective in [`solver/cpsat_matcher.py`](solver/cpsat_matcher.py), (2) a **Bayesian Beta(α,β) peer-reputation** model in [`solver/reputation.py`](solver/reputation.py) with conjugate updates from 1–5 ratings, and (3) a **k-anonymity privacy guard** on rolling rating aggregates in [`solver/privacy.py`](solver/privacy.py). The product is a FastAPI solver microservice ([`api/main.py`](api/main.py)) plus a Next.js 16 + Supabase web app ([`web/`](web/)) with instructor Canvas verification, roster import, peer ratings, and Supabase Realtime live updates. Deployed at <https://packpair.vercel.app> (web) plus a Render-hosted solver; landing page at <https://habibaelswify.github.io/packpair/>.
