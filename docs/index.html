Three AI layers, all central — not a chatbot side-feature

1 · Search (CSP)
Google OR-Tools CP-SAT integer program over a weighted objective (skill diversity + schedule overlap + topic alignment + comm fit). Exact for small classes; greedy fast-path for large.
2 · Reasoning under uncertainty (Bayesian)
Beta(α,β) per-dimension posteriors. Neutral Beta(1,1) prior solves cold-start; conjugate updates from 1–5 peer ratings sharpen the curve.
3 · Privacy (k-anonymity)
Public reputation suppressed until ≥5 ratings back it — prevents re-identification of small-team raters.
